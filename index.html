Research Notes

Research notes are interpretation, with sources listed. They live separately from the catalogue records on the main archive site, and they do not change any catalogue title, place, date, or blurb.

  • Mauch Chunk, the town that sold the slope three times — how a coal town in a gorge sold its gravity railroad as a ride, and then sold its own name.
  • Isles of Shoals, where a garden outlived the hotels — how a fishing town on bare rocks became two rival hotels, and what a garden, a fire, and a conference house left behind.
  • Delaware Water Gap, where the river kept the valley — how hotel keepers turned a river notch into a season, and how a government tried to turn the valley into a lake, and failed.
  • Crawford Notch, where the house outlived the family — how a horse trail, inns and a footpath, and a railroad shelf blasted into a cliff opened one White Mountains gap three times, and what each opening cost the people who built it.
  • Watkins Glen, where every waterfall got a name before it got a staircase — how a newspaper editor named every fall in a gorge and sold tickets to see them, how a painter came to paint one fall and stayed twenty years, and what a flood and a stone rebuild left behind.
